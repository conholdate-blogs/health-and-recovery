      Reference

      History

      One row per recorded run, newest first. Clicks and impressions: Search Console, the 28 days ending on the date under them. Open issues: kinds of problem still open (as in the Issue log). Open items: pages, links and files still affected. Note: what ran since the row before, so a score change from a new check reads as that.

      Site structure

      Tags, categories and the listing pages Hugo builds from them, read from the blog's repo. Hugo builds a listing page for the home page, each category and each tag, in every language, with as many /page/N/ pages as the posts need (N posts a page). A tag with one post is a page with one link: thin, and multiplied by every language. Search: Search Console clicks and impressions over 28 days. Colours: red = alert, green = good, black = in between or a plain count. Tags with one post: red from 50%, green up to 20%; with one or two posts: red from 70%, green up to 35%; tags per post: red above 5, green up to 3; tag share of listing pages: red from 80%, green up to 50%; listing pages beyond page 50: red from 1,000, green at 0; tag URLs in the sitemaps: red if any, green at 0.

      Crawl audit
      Speed

      The lab score (Lighthouse, 0-100) varies by up to ~20 points between runs, so it is shown here only, never as a finding. Whole host: real Chrome users over 28 days, 75th percentile, mobile (good: LCP ≤ 2.5 s, INP ≤ 200 ms, CLS ≤ 0.1). Pages: the most-visited pages per template; "real-user data" counts pages with enough visits for their own numbers.

      AI access

      Blocks on AI training crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, CCBot, Google-Extended) are a business choice, shown here only, never as a finding. Can AI search and answer engines (ChatGPT search, Perplexity, Claude, Google AI Overviews, Copilot) reach each host? "robots.txt" and "Firewall" test their crawlers; a block there is a finding. llms.txt is a proposed convention (llmstxt.org) that some AI tools read: on products, a family counts as covered by its own file or a link from the host's file.

      robots.txt and sitemaps
      Sections

      URLs listed in sitemaps per section, with their languages and hreflang share. Languages: variants found in the URL paths. With hreflang: the share of URLs with hreflang alternates in the sitemap. "In GSC" is what Google reports as discovered from the submitted sitemaps (it counts duplicates).
